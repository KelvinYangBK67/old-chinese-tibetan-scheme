Old Chinese Tibetan Transcription Scheme

A mechanical Tibetan Unicode orthography for Baxter–Sagart Old Chinese reconstructions, based on a normalized slot representation.

Structure

MINOR + TIGHT + ROOT + CLASS(A/B, r, w) + VOWEL + CODA + POSTCODA

decode(encode(BS)) = normalize(BS)

Reconstruction markup is removed. Equivalent P-rV and PrV inputs merge into root P plus an r-feature.

Mapping

Initials use ordinary Tibetan base letters. Minor syllables carry an explicit vowel or ྄; tight preinitials form stacked onsets. Type A, r and w use the eight class-marker combinations documented in the Chinese edition.

Main vowels a A i u e o ə are written Ø, ཱ ི ུ ེ ོ ྀ. Codas and postcodas follow fixed tables.

Reverse conversion

The decoder strips postcoda and coda by longest match, then vowel and class markers, onset stacks, and an optional minor stack. It emits canonical B–S and never recreates editorial brackets.
